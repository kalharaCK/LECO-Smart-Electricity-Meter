# LECO Smart Electricity Meter Portal ⚡
## Complete Project Overview & Feature Guide

---

## 📖 1. What is This Project?

The **LECO Smart Electricity Meter Portal** is a modern, end-to-end digital electricity platform built for **Lanka Electricity Company (LECO)** and its consumers.

In traditional electricity systems, an officer visits each house once a month to read the meter, a paper bill arrives weeks later, and customers have little visibility into how much power they consume daily until they face a shocking bill or sudden disconnection. Furthermore, homes with rooftop solar panels struggle to track how much energy they sell back to the national grid.

This platform modernizes the entire electricity experience by turning electricity into a **smart, transparent, prepaid utility**—just like a mobile phone data plan. Customers can monitor their electricity consumption minute-by-minute, recharge their meters online, track solar exports, and receive proactive warnings before running out of power. At the same time, utility engineers can monitor grid health and manage meters remotely.

## ⚡ 4. Complete Feature Breakdown

### A. Customer Portal Features

#### 1. Live Prepaid Wallet & Balance Projection
- **Real-Time Balance Display**: Shows available cash balance (in Sri Lankan Rupees) that automatically decreases as appliances run.
- **Estimated Days Remaining**: An algorithm calculates average daily burn rate and tells the user how many days the balance will last.
- **Connection Status Badge**: Instantly shows whether power is currently active, running on emergency credit, or suspended.

#### 2. Bidirectional Energy Tracking (Grid Import & Solar Export)
- **Power Imported (In - Red)**: Measures energy drawn from the LECO grid to run household appliances.
- **Power Exported (Out - Green)**: Measures surplus green energy generated by rooftop solar panels fed back into the national grid.
- **Net Energy (In minus Out)**: Automatically computes net units so solar owners (prosumers) can see their exact net credits or payable units.
- **Interactive 7-Day Chart**: Visual day-by-day comparison of solar generation vs. grid consumption.

#### 3. Instant Online Recharge & Digital Receipts
- **Multiple Payment Methods**: Supports Credit/Debit Cards, Bank Transfer, and digital vouchers.
- **Quick Preset Buttons**: 1-tap buttons for common top-up amounts (`Rs. 500`, `Rs. 1,000`, `Rs. 2,000`, `Rs. 5,000`).
- **Cryptographic Recharge Tokens**: Generates 20-digit STS-compliant recharge tokens for physical keypad meters.
- **Digital Tax Invoices & Receipts**: Downloadable and printable receipts with complete transaction IDs, VAT/SSCL tax breakdowns, and date stamps.
- **Payment History Ledger**: Complete log of past recharges with status badges and instant receipt lookup.

#### 4. Official PUCSL Tiered Tariff Engine & Bill Predictor
- Implements Sri Lanka's **PUCSL (Public Utilities Commission)** domestic block tariff structure:
  - Tier 1: 0 – 30 kWh (subsidized lifeline rate)
  - Tier 2: 31 – 60 kWh
  - Tier 3: 61 – 90 kWh
  - Higher Tiers: 91 – 120 kWh, 121 – 180 kWh, 180+ kWh
- **Tier Stepping Alerts**: Shows customers which tariff bracket they are currently in and warns them when they are close to jumping into an expensive tier.
- **Fixed Monthly Charge Automation**: Accurately accounts for fixed regulatory charges based on monthly consumption.

#### 5. Interactive Energy Simulator
- Allows users to simulate power consumption by toggling virtual household appliances (Air Conditioners, Refrigerators, Water Pumps, EV Chargers).
- Instantly shows how turning on an appliance affects the hourly cost, daily burn rate, and end-of-month projected bill.

#### 6. Emergency Lifeline & Consumer Protection Safeguards
- **Emergency Buffer Credit**: If the balance drops to Rs. 0.00, users can activate a Rs. 500 emergency buffer so essential lights and medical equipment remain powered.
- **Night Curfew Friendly Disconnect Rule**: In line with regulatory standards, the system will never cut off power overnight (between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM) or on public Poya holidays, giving families time to recharge in the morning.

#### 7. AI Energy Assistant Bot ("Watts-Up")
- An integrated smart chatbot accessible from any screen.
- Answers questions about electricity tariffs, suggests tips to lower power usage, explains unexpected surges, and helps guide users through recharging or logging complaints.

#### 8. Digital Dispute & Complaints Desk
- **Categorized Issue Logging**: Report meter faults, billing issues, power fluctuations, or physical damage.
- **Live SLA Tracking Stepper**: Tracks tickets through 4 stages: `Logged` → `In Review` → `Engineer Dispatched` → `Resolved`.
- **1-Tap Emergency Hotline (1987)**: Direct button for reporting fallen power lines, fire hazards, or severe emergencies.

#### 9. Multi-Meter Support
- Link multiple meters to a single account (e.g., Home, Parents' Home, Office, Annex).
- Switch between meters seamlessly without creating separate login accounts.

#### 10. Mobile-First App Design
- Complete responsive mobile interface with a **Native Bottom Navigation Dock** (`Overview`, `Recharge`, `History`, `Help`, `Settings`).
- Touch-friendly 44px+ buttons, mobile transaction card feeds (no horizontal table scrolling), and slide-up bottom sheets for chat and receipts.

#### 1. Fleet Overview Dashboard
- High-level bird's-eye view of all smart meters across the jurisdiction.

#### 2. Live Grid Telemetry & Meter Auditing

#### 3. Remote Relay Control (Disconnect / Reconnect)

#### 4. Complaint & Field Dispatch Center
- Technical staff view customer tickets, update investigation notes, assign priority levels (Normal, High, Emergency), and mark issues resolved.

#### 5. User & Meter Management
- Register new smart meters to customer accounts, adjust credit balances, and audit historical transactions.

## 🔄 5. How It Works: The End-to-End Flow

### Scenario 1: Everyday Electricity Consumption & Solar Export
1. The **Smart Meter** at the home measures electricity drawn from the grid (Import) and solar energy sent back (Export).
2. The meter securely reports data to the **Cloud Backend**.
3. The **Tariff Engine** calculates the cost of net units consumed using official PUCSL rates and deducts the balance.
4. The customer sees their balance, solar savings, and remaining days update on their phone in real time.

### Scenario 2: Recharging the Meter
1. The customer receives a notification: *"Your balance is low (below Rs. 300)"*.
2. The customer opens the **Customer Portal**, clicks **"Recharge"**, selects **Rs. 1,000**, and pays.
3. The payment is processed immediately:
   - The meter balance increases by Rs. 1,000.
   - An STS digital token is generated for keypad meters.
   - An SMS and email receipt are dispatched.
   - If the power was suspended, the meter automatically reconnects the relay within seconds.

### Scenario 3: Reporting a Power Problem
1. A resident notices voltage flickering on their street.
2. They open the **Complaints** section, select *"Voltage Fluctuation"*, and submit a ticket.
3. The ticket immediately shows up in the **Staff Portal** complaint queue.
4. The utility officer assigns an engineer, and the customer sees their status change from *"Logged"* to *"Investigating"* in real time.

## 🛡️ 6. Security & Reliability Highlights

- **Role-Based Access Control**: Strict separation between customer accounts and utility staff permissions.
- **Secure Authentication**: Protected by cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT) and secure cookie management.
- **Financial Audit Trails**: Every single recharge and balance deduction is recorded immutably in the database.
- **Fault-Tolerant Architecture**: Uses client-side error boundaries so an issue in one widget never crashes the entire website.
